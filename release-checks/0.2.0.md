## Security and limitations

Checkout audit: **24 moderate affected-package entries**. Final consumer prefix:
**25 moderate entries**, including the plugin's propagated entry; **two** underlying
advisories, `GHSA-3g3m-2fjr-6pw4` and `GHSA-g3hv-75wg-m249`. No high/critical
findings. No forced upgrades, overrides or untested Google metadata downgrade.
See DEPENDENCIES.md for reachability boundaries; transitive does not mean safe.

The unchanged optional-close behavior has regressions and earlier live
background-shell, pending-form and permanent-selection-pin evidence; it was
not enabled for the default-retention packaging acceptance. There is no atomic
Herdr/OpenCode close-vs-click/resume guarantee. Native nested delegation,
cold-process recovery of existing viewers and remote HTTPS Basic auth remain
unverified live. Other platforms/versions are not claimed.

Source/metadata ownership is same-user bookkeeping, not a security boundary.
Custom competing sidebar writers cannot all be detected. Setup preserves or
refuses customized files, uses private journals/backups and requires no concurrent
settings edits. The entire OpenCode executable is outside this source audit.
